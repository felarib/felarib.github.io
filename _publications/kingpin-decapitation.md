excerpt: 'Compares the Primeiro Comando da Capital (São Paulo) and Comando Vermelho (Rio de Janeiro) to ask how criminal governance structure conditions the effects of leader removal. Using an event-study design that distinguishes arrests from police killings, the paper finds that removals shift violence between visible and concealed forms rather than simply raising or lowering its level, with undetermined-intent deaths serving as a proxy for concealment.'
